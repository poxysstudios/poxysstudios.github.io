Common questions

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?
Sign in with Apple in Settings. Your progress is saved to your account, so signing in with the same Apple ID on another device brings it back.

I lost my progress.
If you were signed in, reopen the game while connected to the internet and it should restore. If it doesn't, email us with your leaderboard name and roughly when you last played, and we'll look into it.

A purchase didn't work.
Purchases are processed by Apple, and boosts you buy are saved to your account. If one didn't arrive, reopen the game while connected to the internet and it will be picked up. If it still hasn't appeared, email us with your leaderboard name. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?
In the game, go to Settings → Delete Account. This removes your progress and leaderboard entry permanently. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I stop personalised ads?
Ads only play when you choose to watch one. To stop them being personalised, turn off tracking for the game in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the UK, EU and Switzerland you can also change your choice in the game under Settings → Privacy Choices.

Someone on the leaderboard has an offensive name.
Email us the name and we'll remove it.
