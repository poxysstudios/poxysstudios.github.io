Privacy Policy

Idle Interstellar Tycoon · Last updated 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information Idle Interstellar Tycoon ("the game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The game is made by PoxysStudios, an independent developer. Contact: poxysstudios@gmail.com.

In short: we store your game progress and your leaderboard name and scores. The game has optional ads that only play when you choose to watch one for a reward; they're provided by Google, who may use your device's advertising identifier if you allow it. We never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple gives us an account identifier and, the first time you sign in, your name and email address. You can choose to hide your email, in which case we receive an Apple relay address instead. If you play as a guest, we create an anonymous account with no personal details.
  • Game progress. Your save data (planets, upgrades, currency, settings and similar) and a few recent backups of it, so your progress can be restored on a new device.
  • Leaderboard. Your display name and game stats (such as total energy earned, play time and minigame scores), which are visible to other players. You appear under a random name like "Pilot AB12" unless you choose your own.
  • Purchases. If in-app purchases are offered, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We only receive confirmation of what was bought, never your payment details.

Ads

The game offers rewarded ads: an ad only plays when you tap a button offering a reward for watching it (for example a free boost or a revive). There are no banners and no ads between screens. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

  • What Google collects. To show and measure ads, Google's ad software collects information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), your IP address and the approximate location it suggests, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Your choice on tracking. The first time you choose to watch an ad, iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you allow it, Google can show ads more relevant to you. If you don't, you still get ads, just not personalised ones. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.
  • UK, EU and Switzerland. Players there are asked for consent before ads use their data, through a message provided by Google. You can change your choice later under Settings → Privacy Choices in the game.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics tools of our own, and no tracking beyond the ads described above.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access, and no precise location.
  • We never sell your data or share it with data brokers.

How we use it

To run the game: keeping your progress safe, syncing it between devices, showing the leaderboard, showing the ads you choose to watch, and responding if you contact us for support.

Where it's stored

Game data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. Sign-in is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Game updates are delivered through Expo, which may see basic technical information such as your device type and app version, but no personal details. Ads are provided by Google AdMob under Google's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your save data, backups and leaderboard entry are removed.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the game under Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, saves and leaderboard entry, and revokes the game's access to your Apple ID. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under UK GDPR), you can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or object to how it's used. Email poxysstudios@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority; in the UK that's the ICO.

Children

The game is rated 9+ and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect more than the information described above from anyone, and leaderboard names should not contain personal information. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
